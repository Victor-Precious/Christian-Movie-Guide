A movie can be in more than one topic — just separate them with commas in the Topic column, e.g. Family, Hope. It'll appear on both shelves.

Each topic must exactly match one of these (case-sensitive):
New Releases · Newly found · Salvation · Redemption · Forgiveness/Reconciliation · Repentance/Restoration · Spiritual Enlightenment · Hope · Faith · Consecration · Addictions · Brotherly Love and Sacrifice · Purpose Fulfilment · Deliverance/Spiritual Warfare · Evangelism · Children · Young Adults · Couples · Parents · Ministers · Love Relationship · Family · Old classics

Adding a brand-new topic? You'll also need to add a matching shelf (with the same data-topic value) to your HTML — this tool will still convert it, but it won't show up on the site until that shelf exists.
